A few helpful details

Frequently asked questions

Everything you might want to know before ordering.

How do I place an order?

Choose a design and contact us through WhatsApp. We will guide you through the details and payment process.

Can I request a customised design?

Yes! Send us your idea, preferred style and event details. We will discuss the options with you.

How will I receive my invitation?

Your completed digital invitation will be shared with you online.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery time depends on the design and customisation. We will confirm the expected delivery time when you order.

Can I request a design that is not listed?

Absolutely. Choose Other Services or message us a screenshot/reference of the style you want.

Are all designs editable in Canva?

Only designs specifically listed as Canva templates are intended to be editable. Please confirm before ordering.
